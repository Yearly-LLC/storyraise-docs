2026 Impact Report — What the Numbers Say

The report is getting opened, but most readers are deciding fast: the Welcome Letter absorbs nearly all first clicks and loses many before they reach the impact content. The strongest signal sits with a small group of known constituents who returned, read deeply, and clicked intent links.

What Stood Out

  • The first section is doing too much gatekeeping: 192 of 200 openers started with the Welcome Letter, and 130 dropped there. That means many readers never reached the donor-facing proof points in “Your Gift in Action” or “Programs & Impact.”

  • Readers who got past the opening tended to follow the intended story arc, but the next major leak came at “Programs & Impact,” where 20 of 46 dropped at step 3. That section may be landing as dense or late, even though it is likely one of the pages donors came to see.

  • Engagement is broad but shallow: 249 views translated into just ~2 sections per session, with an estimated 131 seconds per session and 16 seconds per section. People are sampling rather than settling in.

  • Constituent engagement is much more concentrated than general traffic: 8 of 30 identified constituents returned, and all 8 carry 3-star engagement. The best prospects are not just reading; several clicked donate or downloaded the PDF.

Worth a Follow-Up

  • Saoirse Brennan — returned, read 100%, and clicked both the donation link and PDF.
  • Mateo Salinas — returned, read 88%, and clicked the donation link.
  • Henry Aldridge — returned, read 75%, and clicked the donation link.

Next Time

  • Move “Your Gift in Action” or a short impact snapshot ahead of the Welcome Letter, since 130 readers dropped before reaching the proof of impact.

  • Tighten “Programs & Impact” into fewer, more scannable beats or split it with a visual break; losing 20 of 46 there suggests readers need a clearer path through the substance.

  • Send personal follow-up to the 3-star returners before a broad resend; the best next gifts are more likely inside that 8-person group than in the general view count.

stars How Engagement Score Works

Storyraise Engagement Score helps you quickly identify constituents who showed meaningful engagement with your content.

What the stars mean

  • ★★★
    Attention

    Viewers in the upper percentile of read time for this report.

  • ★★★
    Depth

    Above-median read time and viewed more than 50% of the report.

  • ★★★
    Intent

    All of the above plus a follow-up action — a CTA click or return visit.

Intent signals

Intent signals appear as badges next to a constituent's name even when their star score is low. For example, a 10-second viewer who clicks “Donate Now” will still show a Donation CTA badge, so meaningful actions never get hidden by a strict time threshold.

Engagement Score is intended as a directional engagement indicator and should be used alongside your organization's broader constituent insights.
