    Read the transcript

    You sent your impact report. Did it land? Storyraise Analytics shows you who read it, what held their attention, and who might be ready to hear from you.

    Your Home tab shows what's being read across all of your reports. Open any report's Analytics for the full picture, in two views: The Numbers, and The Story.

    Up top, Constituents Opened counts the people on your list who opened their personalized link, and Constituent Returns shows how many came back. Views counts every visit, repeats included. And Average Time Spent is the average reader's total time, across all of their visits.

    Highlights do the first read for you, like when readers peak. And the map shows roughly where your audience is reading from.

    With personalized links, you see names. Most Engaged Constituents ranks your standouts with an Engagement Score. One star means they read longer than the typical constituent on this report. Two stars, they also got through at least half of it. Three stars, they took action too, by clicking a link or coming back. And badges flag key actions, like a donation click, even when the stars don't.

    Where The Clicks Go groups every link click by what readers wanted, from donating to opening a document. Reading shows attention. Clicking shows intent.

    How They Read shows how far people get. Section Retention traces their path from section to section, and the red edge marks where visits ended. A big drop after your opening tells you exactly what to tighten.

    Top Visit Times shows the days and times of day people actually read, so your next send can land when your readers show up.

    Short on time? The Story sums it all up in plain English, with people worth a follow-up and ideas for next time, ready to download and share.

    Remember: anonymous links tell you how your report performed. Personalized links tell you who to call on Monday.

    Turn insights into better stewardship

    Five ways to put what you learn to work.
    1. 1
      Reach out to engaged readers. Someone came back, read deeply, or clicked? Reconnect while interest is fresh, and mention what caught their attention.
    2. 2
      Follow up based on intent. A donation click, a volunteer link, or an event RSVP tells you what to talk about next.
    3. 3
      Build on what resonated. Reuse a high-interest story in an email, a social post, or a donor conversation.
    4. 4
      Start gathering the next story now. Share what worked, and what was missing, with your development, communications, and program teams.
    5. 5
      Look for patterns over time. One week is a snapshot. Three months or a year shows what consistently brings readers back.
