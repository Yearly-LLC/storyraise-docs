Automated donor journeys

Send the right report at the right moment.

Pair a personalized Storyraise report with your email platform's automations. New donors sync in from your CRM, and each one opens a report made just for them, even if they gave long after you published it.

Watch the video Set it up
    Read the transcript

    A first-time donor gives on a Tuesday. Two weeks later, a welcome arrives with a report made just for them. And nobody on your team had to press send.

    It takes three things you may already have: your CRM, a personalized Storyraise report, and an email platform with automations or journeys.

    Start with one report. Merge tags, like a first name, fill in differently for every donor who opens it. You publish it once.

    Next, connect your CRM in the Connections tab, and turn on Auto-sync. New donors in your CRM become Storyraise constituents on their own, with no uploads and no one opening the dashboard.

    Now choose Send in an email on your report. Paste your email platform's merge tag for the donor's email address, and copy the link. Every email carries that one link, and each donor's copy fills in with their own address.

    In your email platform, build the journey the way you already do. A first gift starts it, it waits two weeks, then it sends a welcome email with a button that uses your link. Your platform decides who gets it, and when.

    When a donor clicks, Storyraise looks them up right then, and the report greets them by name. That includes donors who joined long after you published, so there's nothing to republish.

    And because every link is personal, Analytics shows you who read their welcome, by name, so you know who to thank next.

    The same setup works for other moments, too: a giving anniversary, a year-end impact report, or a check-in with a donor you haven't heard from.

    The step-by-step setup is right here on this page.

    How it works

    Three pieces you may already have, working together.

    Your CRM keeps donors current

    Connect it in the Connections tab and turn on Auto-sync. New and updated donors come in on the schedule you choose, daily or weekly.

    One personalized report

    Add merge tags like @@first_name@@ and publish once. Each donor's copy fills in the moment they open their link.

    Your email platform sends it

    Build the journey where you already email donors. Your platform decides who gets the report, and when.

    Set it up

    Five steps, and then the journey runs on its own.
    1. 1
      Connect your CRM and turn on Auto-sync.

      In Storyraise, open Connections, find your CRM under Add a connection, and click Connect. Then open the connection's More menu, switch on Auto-sync, and pick a Frequency. Managing connections

    2. 2
      Personalize and publish your report.

      Type merge tags such as @@first_name@@ wherever you want a donor's details, using the field names on your constituent records. Then publish. Personalized links

    3. 3
      Copy the personalized link.

      On the published report, choose Send in an email. Under Email tool, pick Mailchimp, or choose Other and paste your platform's merge tag for the contact's email address. Then click Copy link.

    4. 4
      Build the journey in your email platform.

      Create an automation or journey that starts from the moment you care about, such as a first gift. Add a wait if you like, then send an email whose button uses the link you copied.

    5. 5
      Test it with a real record.

      Send a test to someone who is also a constituent in Storyraise, and open the link. The report should greet them by name.

    Timing. A donor's record needs to reach Storyraise before they click. A daily sync is comfortably ahead of any wait of a day or more. Data refreshes

    Raiser's Edge NXT and Slate. Run Sync now once after turning on Auto-sync, so Storyraise knows which list or column mapping to bring in each time.

    Merge tags for popular email platforms

    Paste your report's link, find your email platform, and copy a link that fills in each donor's email address when your platform sends.

    • Acoustic CampaignMarketing automation
      %%Email%%

      The name between the %% signs must match your database's email field.

    • Action NetworkNonprofit CRM and fundraising
      {{ Email }}

      Insert Email from the clips menu to confirm the exact spelling.

    • ActiveCampaignMarketing automation
      %EMAIL%
    • Adobe Marketo EngageMarketing automation
      {{lead.Email Address}}

      The tag includes a space. Copy it exactly.

    • AWeberEmail marketing
      {{ subscriber.email }}

      AWeber doesn't track clicks on links that contain personalization.

    • beehiivEmail marketing
      {{email}}

      Case-sensitive.

    • Bonterra EveryActionNonprofit CRM and fundraising
      {{SubscriberEmail}}

      For Targeted Email. Bonterra advises against putting email addresses in links, so check with your admin first.

    • BrazeMarketing automation
      {{${email_address}}}
    • BrevoEmail marketing
      {{ contact.EMAIL }}

      EMAIL must match the attribute name on your Contact attributes page.

    • Campaign MonitorEmail marketing
      [email]
    • CiviCRMNonprofit CRM and fundraising
      {contact.email_primary.email}

      Older versions use {contact.email}.

    • Constant ContactEmail marketing
      [[emailAddress]]

      Dynamic links don't fill in on previews or test sends. Send a copy of the email to yourself to check.

    • Constant Contact Lead Gen & CRMMarketing automation
      {$emailAddress}

      Formerly SharpSpring.

    • Customer.ioMarketing automation
      {{customer.email}}

      Case-sensitive.

    • DripMarketing automation
      {{ subscriber.email }}

      Use subscriber.email. In Drip, {{ email }} refers to the email itself, not the recipient.

    • EmmaEmail marketing
      [% member:email default="" %]

      Emma's docs don't show this tag inside a link, so send yourself a real test first.

    • FlodeskEmail marketing
      {{ subscriber.email }}
    • GetResponseEmail marketing
      {{CONTACT `subscriber_email`}}

      This is GetResponse's form for links. The backticks are part of the tag.

    • HubSpotMarketing automation
      {{contact.email}}

      In marketing emails, add the link with Personalize in the button or link URL field. Leave no spaces inside the braces.

    • IterableMarketing automation
      {{email}}

      Case-sensitive.

    • KeapMarketing automation
      ~Contact.Email~
    • KitEmail marketing
      {{ subscriber.email_address | url_encode }}

      Kit recommends the url_encode filter for tags inside links.

    • KlaviyoMarketing automation
      {{ email }}

      Case-sensitive.

    • Little Green LightNonprofit CRM and fundraising
      [[email]]

      Paste the link into the HTML source or the link builder, so the brackets aren't converted.

    • MailchimpEmail marketing
      *|EMAIL|*

      Case-sensitive. Storyraise's Send in an email builds this link for you.

    • Mailchimp TransactionalEmail marketing
      *|EMAIL|*

      Works when your merge language is set to Mailchimp.

    • MailerLiteEmail marketing
      {$email}

      Case-sensitive. MailerLite's docs don't show this tag inside a link, so send yourself a real test first.

    • Marketing Cloud Account EngagementMarketing automation
      {{{Recipient.Email}}}

      For accounts on Handlebars merge language. Insert the recipient's email from the merge field picker to confirm the exact tag.

    • MoosendEmail marketing
      #recipient:email#

      Moosend's docs don't show this tag inside a link, so send yourself a real test first.

    • NationBuilderNonprofit CRM and fundraising
      {{ recipient.email }}
    • OmnisendEmail marketing
      [[contact.email]]
    • OntraportMarketing automation
      [Email]

      Case-sensitive. Pick Email from the merge field dropdown to confirm the exact tag.

    • Salesforce Marketing Cloud EngagementMarketing automation
      %%emailaddr%%

      Salesforce advises against putting email addresses in links, so check with your Marketing Cloud admin first.

    • Twilio SendGridEmail marketing
      {{email}}

      For Marketing Campaigns. Type the tag straight into the link's URL field.

    • WildApricotNonprofit CRM and fundraising
      {Contact_Email}

      Case-sensitive. WildApricot's docs don't show macros inside links, so send yourself a real test first.

    Sending from your CRM's own email tool, such as Bloomerang, Raiser's Edge NXT, Neon CRM, Virtuous, or Givebutter? Insert the email address field from its merge field menu, then paste exactly what it adds.

    Each tag comes from that platform's own documentation, checked September 2026. Copy a tag exactly as shown, since a mistyped tag looks fine but won't personalize, and send yourself a test before a big send. In Storyraise, Send in an email builds the same link for you.

    Journeys worth building

    The same setup works for any moment your email platform can start a journey from.

    Giving anniversary

    On the anniversary of a donor's first gift, show them what a year of their support made possible.

    Year-end impact report

    Send every donor this year's report, each one opening with their own name and the details you merged in.

    Lapsed donor check-in

    When a donor hasn't given in a while, remind them of the difference they made, by name.

    Know who opened their welcome

    Every link is personal, so Analytics shows you who read their report, by name, and who might be ready to hear from you next.

    See it in action on the Storyraise Analytics page.

    Ready to build your first journey?

    Connect your CRM in Storyraise, then choose Send in an email on any published report.
