# Creating a video message

A Storyraise Video message is one short recording, automatically personalized into a unique video for every recipient. This page is the end-to-end overview; the pages that follow go deeper on each step.

## Where to start

In the dashboard, open **Videos** in the sidebar and click **Add new** (or click **New video** on Home). Storyraise Video opens in a new tab with a fresh video. Video is licensed separately; if your account doesn't have it, **New video** on Home is dimmed and shows **Contact us for access**.

## The flow at a glance

1. **Choose a message type** — what kind of message you're sending.
2. **Add recipients** — from your constituents or a CSV. You can **Skip for now** and add them later.
3. **Record your message** — webcam or upload, with auto-generated talking points.
4. **Edit your scenes** — the personalized text that appears as you speak.
5. **Finish the page and message** — **Edit template** (text and logo on the viewing page), **Add attachments** (a report or a link), and **Edit message content** (the text recipients get with their link).
6. **Preview and send** — check each person's version, then deliver.

## 1. Choose your message type

Start with **What kind of message are you sending?** and pick the one that fits:

- **Thank You!**
- **Case for Giving**
- **Make an Appeal**
- **Invitation**
- **Event Recap**
- **Just an Update**

Your choice tailors the suggested talking points and the starting scene layouts, so you're never staring at a blank screen.

## 2–6. Add recipients, build, personalize, and send

From there you'll choose who receives it, record, refine the on-screen scenes, and send. Each step has its own guide:

- [Sending and recipients](sending-and-recipients.md) — recipients, per-person preview, and delivery.
- [Recording your video](recording-your-video.md) — capturing or uploading your footage, plus automatic captions.
- [Scenes and personalization](scenes-and-personalization.md) — the personalized text overlays and merge tags.

## Tips for a great message

- **Keep it short.** Aim for 45–60 seconds. The personalization, not the length, is what makes it land.
- **Talk to one person.** Even though hundreds receive it, record as if you're speaking to a single supporter — because each of them sees a version made for them.
- **Lead with warmth.** A genuine thank-you in the first few seconds earns the rest of the watch.
