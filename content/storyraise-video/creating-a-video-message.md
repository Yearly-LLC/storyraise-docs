# Creating a video message

Start a video from the Videos list, pick a template, choose who it's for, and record. The editor then takes you through five short steps to a page you can send.

## Where to start

In the dashboard, open **Videos** in the sidebar and click **New video**. Storyraise asks **What are you making?**:

- **Thank a constituent** — a quick, personal thank-you to one person. See [Thanking a constituent](thanking-a-constituent.md).
- **Start from scratch** — appeals, updates, events, or anything else. Record or upload anything. The editor opens in a new tab.

![The What are you making? window with two choices: Thank a constituent (pick a constituent and we write the script from their last gift) and Start from scratch (appeals, updates, events), and a Cancel button](/assets/img/howto/video_new_chooser.png)

You can also click **New video** on Home, which goes straight to **Start from scratch**. Video is licensed separately; if your account doesn't have it, **New video** on Home is dimmed and shows **Contact us for access**.

## 1. Pick a template

The first screen is **Pick a template**. Each one starts in your Brand Kit look and shapes the scenes Storyraise writes for you:

- **Thank-you** — a warm, personal thanks, with light scenes that keep your face on screen.
- **Appeal** — name the need, show one number, make a clear ask.
- **Impact update** — results first, with big numbers on side cards and a thank-you to close.
- **Event recap** — names, moments, and the date, with a lower third for every person named.
- **Social clip** — a hook in the first second and short, punchy lines. It opens in the Phone shape; Square and Wide are one click away.

Templates your team saved appear here too, marked **Your org**. Pick one and click **Continue**.

## 2. Add your recipients

Next, **Now let's add your recipients.** Choose how:

- **Select Recipients** — search your organization's constituents and tick the people you want.
- **Choose a List** — add everyone on one of your Constituents lists, or all constituents.

Not ready? Click **Skip for now**. You can add people later from **Share** (see [Sending, sharing, and downloading](sending-and-recipients.md)).

## 3. Get ready to record

The last setup screen has a few recording tips — good lighting, a clean background, camera at eye level. Click **Let's Go!** to open the editor.

## The editor


![The video editor: All videos, the title, Share and Download along the top; Preview as and Wide, Phone, Square above the stage, which shows the constituent page with the sender, the video and the words under it; and the five steps under Your video on the right, with Record your video open](/assets/img/howto/video_editor.png)

The stage on the left shows your video. The steps on the right, under **Your video**, go in order, and each one ends with a **Next** button that names the step after it. Until you've recorded, the later steps say **Record first**.

1. **Record your video** — webcam or upload, with talking points. See [Recording your video](recording-your-video.md).
2. **Edit scenes** — the words over your video, your music and your look. See [Scenes and personalization](scenes-and-personalization.md).
3. **Edit captions** — fix anything the transcript got wrong. See [Recording your video](recording-your-video.md).
4. **Your page** — the page each constituent opens. It's ready as is; you can add more later.
5. **Results** — who watched, how far they got, and where people stopped. See [Video analytics](../distribution-and-engagement/video-analytics.md).

Along the top of the editor:

- **All videos** takes you back to the Videos list. The title next to it has a pencil to rename the video.
- **Wide**, **Phone**, and **Square** above the stage pick the shape of your downloaded file and social posts. Your page plays the video in the shape you recorded it.
- **Share** sends the video or copies a link. **Download** makes a video file. Both are covered in [Sending, sharing, and downloading](sending-and-recipients.md).

## 4. Your page

**Your page** is what each constituent sees when they open their link. When your video is recorded, the step opens on a line that tells you where you stand — for example, **Ready to send.** Constituents can watch your video now — with **Send to constituents**, **Copy link**, and **Download** right under it.

![The Your page step: Ready to send. Constituents can watch your video now, with Send to constituents, Copy link and Download; Words under your video with a Help Fill Our Shelves heading; Page details reading No button, No logo; and Design your page (optional)](/assets/img/howto/video_your_page.png)

- **Preview as** above the stage shows the page as a particular constituent will see it, with their name filled in.
- **Words under your video** is the text below the player.
- **Page details** holds the rest: what shows under the video, who's talking (your name, title, and photo), the logo and colors (**Use Brand Kit logo**, **Use Brand Kit colors**), and the button — **Reply to** followed by your first name (a reply by email), **A Collect form**, **A published report**, **A donate link**, or **No button**.
- **Design your page (optional)** opens the page in the report builder if you want to add more around your video. The page stays a draft until you publish it there; constituents can watch your video either way.

If the page still has placeholder text, a banner says **Your page still has placeholder text, so the link is on hold.** Click **Edit the text** or **Remove placeholder** to clear it.

## Tips for a great message

- **Keep it short.** Aim for 45–60 seconds. The personalization, not the length, is what makes it land.
- **Talk to one person.** Even though many people receive it, record as if you're speaking to a single supporter — each of them sees a version made for them.
- **Lead with warmth.** A genuine thank-you in the first few seconds earns the rest of the watch.
