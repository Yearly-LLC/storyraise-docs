# Recording your video

Record with your webcam or upload a video, read from a teleprompter if you like, and trim the ends. Storyraise writes the captions for you.

## Record or upload

The first step in the editor is **Record your video**. Before anything is recorded, the stage reads **No video yet** with two buttons:

- **Record with webcam** — record in your browser, no software to install.
- **Upload a video** — use a video you've already made, for example one recorded on your phone.

## The recorder

**Record with webcam** opens **Record your video**:

- **Your talking points** — Storyraise writes a short script from your template and your organization, sized for a 45–60 second message. It runs on the teleprompter over the camera view; use **Speed**, **Play**, and **Top** to control it, or **Edit script** to change the words.
- **Wide**, **Phone**, and **Square** pick the shape you record in. A laptop webcam records wide, so a Phone or Square version is a little soft — fine for email and your page.
- **Background** blurs or replaces what's behind you — **Light blur**, **Strong blur**, **Brand color**, **Gradient**, **Office**, **Bookshelf**, or **Your image**.
- **Framing tips** appear over the camera view while it's on, naming the one thing worth fixing — for example, **Leave a little room above your head. Tilt the camera up a touch.** or **Move toward the center of the frame.** When you're set, it says **Framing looks good.** The camera image never leaves your computer for this.
- **Camera and mic** picks your camera and microphone, and has switches for **Studio mic** (for a good external mic with headphones), **Framing grid**, and **Teleprompter**.
- Click **Start recording** when you're ready.

### Record on your phone

A laptop webcam records wide, so a Phone-shaped video keeps only a narrow slice of it. For a sharp Phone video, click **Want a sharper Phone video? Record it on your phone**. **Record on your phone for a sharp Phone video** shows a QR code:

1. Scan it with your phone's camera.
2. Sign in if it asks, then hold the phone upright and record.
3. Save. The take lands in this video.

If scanning doesn't work, copy the link and open it on your phone instead. **Record with my webcam** goes back to the webcam.

## Recording tips

A few seconds of setup makes a big difference:

- **Lighting** — face a window or light source; avoid a bright window *behind* you.
- **Background** — a clean wall or tidy space keeps the focus on you.
- **Camera height** — at eye level, with you centered in the frame.
- **Sound** — record somewhere quiet; built-in mics pick up echo and traffic.
- **Phone or tablet** works great if its camera is better than your computer's — record there with **Record it on your phone**, or upload the file with **Upload a video**.

## Trim, re-record, or replace


Once your video is in, the step says **Your video is in. Trim it, or record again.**

![The Record your video step after recording: Your video is in. Trim it, or record again, with Record again, Replace video, Trim start and end, a delete button and Next: Edit scenes](/assets/img/howto/video_record_step.png)

- **Record again** opens the recorder for a new take.
- **Replace video** uploads a different file.
- **Trim start and end** shows the recording strip; drag the teal handles to cut the start and end.

When you're happy, click **Next: Edit scenes**.

## Automatic transcription and captions

After you record, Storyraise automatically transcribes your video, breaks the words into natural, readable caption lines synced to your speech, and captures a thumbnail for emails.

Captions matter: many people watch with the sound off, and captions make your message accessible to viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing. Constituents can turn captions on or off while watching.

### Edit captions


The third step, **Edit captions**, lets you fix anything the transcript got wrong. Click a line to jump the video there.

![The Edit captions step with Captions and Transcript tabs and three timed caption lines. The first line reads Redfield where the speaker said Wrenfield, the kind of slip to fix here. Below are Download captions (.srt) and Start over from the transcript](/assets/img/howto/video_edit_captions.png)

- **Captions** — edit each line's words. **Download captions (.srt)** saves a caption file, and **Start over from the transcript** rebuilds the lines if you want a fresh start.
- **Transcript** — every word you said. Select words, then press Delete or click **Cut** to cut them from the video; click struck-out words to restore them. **Remove pauses over 0.8s** and **Remove filler words** (um, uh, like, you know) tidy the whole take at once.
