# Managing your videos

The Videos list puts what needs doing first, keeps unrecorded drafts out of the way, and lets you rename, duplicate, archive, and tidy up.

Open **Videos** in the dashboard sidebar. Along the top are the views — **Videos**, **My constituents**, **Team this week** (for Owners and Admins), and **Campaigns** — and the search box, the filters, and **New video**. The other views are covered in [Video analytics](../distribution-and-engagement/video-analytics.md).

## What's on the list

Recorded videos show as cards with their thumbnail, in two groups:

- **To do** — videos waiting on someone to review them, and videos that are **Ready to send** and were made or played in the last 30 days.
- **All videos** — everything else you've recorded.

Each card shows the title, who it's for (**For Maria Lopez**), and one date — for example, **Created 1 week ago**, or **Viewed 2 days ago** once it's been played or shared. A card that's been played also shows its views. The line at the bottom of a card is the next thing to do:

- **Review** — someone asked you to review it.
- **Send to constituents** — it's recorded and ready.
- **See who watched** — it's gone out; this opens its Results.

## Statuses

Every video has one of these statuses, and the status filter shows how many you have of each:

- **Not recorded** — a draft with no recording yet.
- **Ready to send** — recorded, not sent yet.
- **Sent** — it's gone to at least one person.
- **Watched** — at least one person it was sent to watched half or more.
- **Needs review** — waiting for a teammate's approval (only if your organization uses approvals). It may say **Changes requested** instead.

## Not recorded yet

Drafts without a recording sit in a folded-away section at the bottom, **Not recorded yet**, so they don't crowd the list. If you started thank-yous this week and haven't recorded them, a line near the top says so — click **Show them** to jump there.

Each draft is a row with **Record**, which opens it in the editor. When there are several drafts for the same person, they're grouped into one row (for example, "6 drafts for Maria Lopez"), and its **⋯** menu offers to archive them all (for example, **Archive all 6 drafts**) or to keep the newest and archive the rest (**Keep the newest, archive 5**).

### Tidy up

When old drafts and extra copies pile up, **Tidy up** appears at the foot of the section with a count. It explains what it will archive — drafts started over a week ago, and extra copies for a person who has a newer one — then click **Archive** to confirm or **Cancel**. Each person keeps their newest draft from the past week, and you can restore any of them from Archived.

## Search and filter

- **Search videos** finds videos by title or by who they're for. A search that matches a draft opens **Not recorded yet** so you can see it.
- The first filter shows **All videos** or **My videos**. With approvals turned on it also has **Needs my review** and **Assigned to me**, and once you've archived something, **Archived**.
- **All statuses** narrows the list to one status.

On a phone, both filters live behind the **Filter** button.

## The ⋯ menu

Each card's **⋯** menu has:

- **Send to constituents** (for a video that's ready) — opens the video's Send list in the editor.
- **Edit video** — opens it in the editor in a new tab.
- **See who watched** — its Results.
- **Open constituent page** — the page people see, in a new tab.
- **Copy email link** — a link to paste into your own email. See [Sending, sharing, and downloading](sending-and-recipients.md).
- **Rename** — type a new name and press Enter (Esc cancels).
- **Duplicate** — makes a copy and opens it in a new tab.
- **Archive** — hides the video from your list.

## Archive, Undo, and Restore

Archiving never deletes a video. Right after you archive, a message says **Archived** with an **Undo** button. Later, choose **Archived** in the first filter to see everything you've archived, and click **Restore** on a card to put it back.
