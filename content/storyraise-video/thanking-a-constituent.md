# Thanking a constituent

A quick thank-you is a short video for one person. Pick them, read a script filled from their last gift, record, and send their own link. About two minutes.

## Pick who you're thanking

In the dashboard, open **Videos**, click **New video**, and choose **Thank a constituent**. **Who are you thanking?** lists people worth thanking now — new gifts, upgrades, and constituents at risk — from [Donor Signals](/donor-signals/). Anyone you've already thanked this week is marked **thanked this week**. To thank someone else, search by name or email.

To count it toward a campaign, choose one under **Campaign** first so the thank-you counts toward it.

Click a name and the recorder opens for them. If you'd already started a thank-you for that person and not recorded it, Storyraise picks up that draft instead of making another.

## Record

The panel shows who you're thanking and their last gift. Under **What to say** is a short script filled from their record — on your computer; nothing about the gift is sent to AI. Edit it however you like. If part of it is highlighted, replace it with your own words or delete it before you record.

Click the record button, which carries their first name (for example, **Record Maria's thank-you**), or **Upload a video instead**. In the recorder, **Personalize talking points** refills the script for this person, and **Polish with AI (first name only)** smooths the wording using only their first name.

## Send it

Once you've recorded, you have a few ways to get it to them:

- **Copy for email** — copies a preview that plays in the inbox, linked to their page. Paste it into your own email.
- **Copy Maria's link** (with their first name) — copies their own link.
- **Send with Storyraise Send** — opens [Storyraise Send](../distribution-and-engagement/email-distribution.md) with the email ready and them picked.
- **Open in Gmail**, **Open in Outlook**, or **Mail app** — opens a draft addressed to them. If there's no email on their record, add it in the draft.

**Record again**, **Trim**, and **Other links** sit below.

## Thank the next person

Click **Next constituent** to move straight to the next person on the list, or **Pick another constituent**. The **Who to thank** list on the side shows everyone suggested this week and who's done, and the footer counts how many you've sent and how many were watched this week.

## Afterward

Each thank-you shows up in your Videos list and in **My constituents**, with whether they watched. When someone watches at least half, they're marked **Call next** — a good time to pick up the phone. See [Video analytics](../distribution-and-engagement/video-analytics.md).
