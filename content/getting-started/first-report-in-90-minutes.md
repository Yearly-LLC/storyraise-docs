# Your first report in 90 minutes

If you have a deadline and no designer, this is the path. It skips everything optional and gets a real, publishable report out the door.

You do not need to connect your CRM, import constituents, personalize anything, or learn merge tags to publish your first report. All of that can wait.

## The budget

| Step | Time | What you're doing |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pick a template | 5 min | [Choose one that matches your goal](understanding-templates.md), not one that looks prettiest |
| 2. Set your brand | 10 min | Logo and two colors in your [brand kit](../building-reports/brand-kit.md). Stop there |
| 3. Delete what you can't fill | 5 min | Remove sections you have no content for |
| 4. Write your words | 40 min | The letter and two stories — see [What goes in an annual report](what-goes-in-an-annual-report.md) |
| 5. Add photos | 15 min | [Phone photos are fine](../building-reports/image-sizes-and-dimensions.md) |
| 6. Check it on a phone | 10 min | [Switch to mobile view](../building-reports/mobile-optimization.md) and read it top to bottom |
| 7. Publish | 5 min | [Publish and copy the link](publishing-and-sharing.md) |

Ninety minutes is realistic for a first report on a template you don't customize heavily. A polished annual report with original photography and a full donor list is a longer job — but you can publish this one, then keep editing and republish.

## What to skip your first time

You have permission to ignore all of this:

- **Connecting your CRM.** It's a separate project, usually with your database admin. A report doesn't need it.
- **Importing constituents.** Only needed if you want to personalize or track who read it.
- **Personalized links and merge tags.** Powerful, and not a first-report feature.
- **Custom fonts and a full color palette.** Logo and two colors is enough to look intentional.
- **Infographics and charts.** One or two big numbers as text beats a chart you fought with.
- **Polls, embedded forms, and video.** Later.
- **A custom sending domain.** Only matters when you start emailing from Storyraise.

## The one rule

**Delete more than you add.** Templates ship with more sections than most organizations can fill well. A short report that is finished and true beats a long one with placeholder text in it. If you can't fill a section with something real, remove it.

## When you have more time

Once this one is out, the natural next steps are:

1. [Import your constituents](../crm-and-data/importing-constituent-data.md) so you can see who's reading.
2. [Personalize the report](../distribution-and-engagement/personalized-links.md) so each donor sees their own name.
3. [Look at your analytics](../distribution-and-engagement/acting-on-analytics.md) and follow up with the people who engaged.
