# Organization settings

Your organization is the shared workspace that holds your reports, constituents, brand kit, and team. This page covers the organization-level settings in your account area.

## Your organization info

Open **Account** and stay on the **General** tab. Under **Your Org Info** you'll find:

- **Organization Name** — how your organization is identified across Storyraise. Owners and Admins (anyone who can edit organization settings) can change it here and click **Save name**.
- **Custom URL** — your reports' web address, `your-organization.yearly.report`. This address appears in every report link you've shared, so changing it would break links already sent. It isn't self-service: click **Ask us to change it** and we'll be in touch and take care of the links.

## Your brand

Most of what makes your organization *look* like your organization lives in the **brand kit** — brand colors, report palettes, and font pairs that every new report starts from, plus logos and photos for the builder. See [Brand kit](../building-reports/brand-kit.md).

## Nonprofit certificate

Owners and Admins can upload your nonprofit certificate under **Nonprofit Certificate** (**Add docs** — PDF or DOC). This verifies your nonprofit status with Storyraise. Upload it once; you can replace or remove it later.

## Sending email from your domain

To send email from your own domain (so messages come from an address like `giving@yourdomain.org`), set up your sending domain in Storyraise Send. See [Setting up a custom sending domain](custom-sending-domain.md).

## Belonging to more than one organization

If you work with multiple organizations on Storyraise — say, a foundation and a fiscally sponsored project — you can belong to several at once. Use the **Domain** menu in the sidebar, above your organization name, to switch between them; each has its own reports, constituents, and settings. To choose which one you land on when you sign in, set **Default organization** under **Your Info** (see [Your profile and security](profile-and-security.md)).
