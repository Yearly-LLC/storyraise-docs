# Adding sections

Reports are built from **blocks** — an intro, a story, a stats spread, a donor list. Each block has its own layout and purpose, and you choose from a range of **block types** as you build. (A **section** is a chapter of your report, made up of one or more blocks; sections are what appear in your report's navigation.) This page covers the block types available and how to add them.

## Add a block

1. To start a new section, click the **+** (**Add new section**) button under the section thumbnails on the left. It creates an empty section called "Untitled" and opens it. To add to the section you're in, skip this step.
2. Open the **Blocks** tab in the sidebar on the right. Each block type comes with a short description.
3. Drag a block onto the canvas. It's added with placeholder content ready to edit. A section can hold as many blocks as you like.

You can also let AI draft a block for you — describe what it's about and Storyraise builds an outline. See [AI content generation](ai-content-generation.md).

## Block types

**Openers and narrative**

- **Intro** — set the stage with a captivating title for your report
- **Letter** — heartfelt messages from your organization's leaders
- **Story** — spotlight your organization's compelling stories
- **Prose** — dive deep into topics with detailed text passages
- **Highlight** — draw attention to important content with a striking image-and-text pairing

**Visual**

- **Gallery** — showcase images in a sleek, customizable photo gallery
- **Video** — set the stage with captivating footage

**Numbers and data**

- **Stats** — showcase achievements using key figures
- **Infographics** — infographics with lists, sequences, and comparisons (see [Creating infographics](creating-infographics.md))
- **Poll/Quiz** — ask readers a question, or quiz them on the answer, on a page of its own (see [Adding a poll](adding-a-poll.md) and [Adding a quiz](adding-a-quiz.md))

**People and gratitude**

- **Testimonial** — share praise from supporters to boost credibility
- **People** — introduce your team to build trust
- **Donor List** — acknowledge and celebrate your contributors
- **Thank-You Note** — express gratitude directly

**Action**

- **Donate** — encourage support by linking to your donation form

**Flexible**

- **Basic** — a flexible container with column-based layouts
- **Custom Design** — compose a canvas of photos, text, and shapes
- **Repeater** — create repeating blocks and elements, useful for lists of similar items

> **Note:** **Repeater** needs a Precision plan with Storyraise Collect active. It won't appear in the block list otherwise.

## Managing blocks

- **Duplicate** or **delete** a single block: select it and use the buttons on [the element bar](element-bar.md). Deleted something by mistake? Use **Undo** in the top toolbar.
- **Duplicate** or **delete** a whole section: open the **⋯** menu at the top of the builder and choose **Duplicate section** or **Delete section**.
- **Reorder** your blocks and sections — see [Reordering sections](reordering-sections.md).
- **Hide** a section from the report's navigation menu while keeping it in the report — see [Navigation options](navigation-options.md).

## Tips

- **One idea per block.** Readers scroll a block at a time; a block that tries to do two jobs usually does neither well.
- **Vary the rhythm.** Alternate text-heavy blocks (Letter, Prose) with visual ones (Gallery, Stats) to keep readers moving.
