# Starting a report on brand

Every way of creating a report asks which of your brand kit's palettes and font pairs to use. Your defaults are already selected, so most of the time you just name the report and click create. This page covers what you'll see and what each choice does.

## Where you choose

On the **Reports** page, click **Add new**:

![The Add new menu with From a template, Starter report, Empty report and Folder](/assets/img/howto/brandkit_add_new.png)

- **From a template** — browse finished designs. Pick one and your palette and fonts are applied to it.
- **Starter report** — a simple layout with sample content to build on.
- **Empty report** — no sections, for building from scratch.

All three show **Colors** choice cards before the report is created, plus **Fonts** cards once you've saved a font pair.

## Starter and empty reports

![The New report panel for a Starter report: a Title field; Colors cards for Harbor classic (Default, selected), Sunrise and Standard colors; Fonts cards for Classic (Default, selected), Modern and Storyraise fonts; and the line "Harbor classic and Classic will be applied."](/assets/img/howto/brandkit_starter_panel.png)

1. Type a **Title** (if you leave it blank, it's called "Untitled report").
2. Under **Colors**, keep your default palette or pick another saved palette. Choose **Standard colors** to use Storyraise's built-in colors instead.
3. Under **Fonts**, keep your default pair or pick another. Choose **Storyraise fonts** to skip your brand fonts.
4. Check the line above the button. It says exactly what will be applied, such as "Harbor classic and QA type will be applied."
5. Click **Create report**.

For a starter report, the preview on the left updates as you change the cards. An empty report has no preview because it has no sections yet. Your choices become the report's styles when it opens, so every block you add comes in on brand.

![The New report panel for an Empty report: "Empty report — no sections. Your colors and fonts are applied when it opens."](/assets/img/howto/brandkit_empty_prompt.png)

**Edit in Brand Kit** opens the brand kit in a new tab, if you want to add a palette or pair first.

## Templates

Open a template in the gallery and the detail panel shows the same choices. Here, the alternative to your brand is the template's own look: **Template colors** and **Template fonts**, both marked **As designed**.

![A template detail panel with the Harbor classic palette and Classic fonts selected. The preview is recolored navy and shows a "Shown in your fonts" badge](/assets/img/howto/brandkit_template_brand.jpg)

The preview shows the template with your choices applied, and a **Shown in your fonts** badge appears while your fonts are in use. Click **Template colors** to compare it with the original:

![The same template with Template colors selected: the preview returns to its original purple and the summary reads "Classic will be applied."](/assets/img/howto/brandkit_template_own_colors.jpg)

When you click **Use this template**, your palette is mapped onto the template's design — dark areas take your dark color, buttons take your button color, and so on — and the copy opens in the builder already on brand. **Open full preview** opens a full-page preview that uses the same choices.

### Templates that keep their own colors

A few templates are designed around fixed colors and can't be recolored. For those, the Colors cards are replaced with a note:

![A template panel showing "This template keeps its own colors, so your palette can't be applied. Your brand colors are still pinned in the builder." above the Fonts cards](/assets/img/howto/brandkit_template_keeps_colors.png)

Your fonts can still be applied, and once you're in the builder you can still color any element with your brand colors from its color picker.

## If your brand kit is empty

With no brand colors, palettes, or font pairs, there's nothing to apply yet. The panel offers only **Standard colors**, hides the Fonts cards, and says **Your brand isn't applied to this report.**

![The New report panel with only a Standard colors card and the message "Your brand isn't applied to this report."](/assets/img/howto/brandkit_starter_no_brand.png)

The report is still created normally — with Storyraise's standard colors and fonts. Set up your [brand kit](brand-kit.md) and the next report you create will start on brand.

> **Have brand colors but no palette yet?** Storyraise offers a **Your brand colors** card built automatically from them. Saving a [palette](report-palettes.md) gives you control over exactly which color goes where.

## What gets applied

| You pick… | What happens |
|---|---|
| A palette | Sections, buttons, and accents are colored from it, and button text uses whichever palette color reads better on the button. The palette becomes the report's **Report palette** in the builder. |
| A font pair | Its heading and body fonts become the report's heading and body fonts. |
| Standard colors / Template colors | The report keeps the starter's or template's own colors. |
| Storyraise fonts / Template fonts | The report keeps the starter's or template's own fonts. On an empty report, Storyraise fonts means a plain sans-serif. |

The report takes its own copy of these choices. Later changes to the brand kit don't change it.

Your logo isn't placed automatically. Add it in the builder from the sidebar's **Assets** tab, under **Your Logos**.

## Changing your mind later

- **Colors:** in the builder, open **⋯ → Styles → More color palettes** and pick any palette under **Your Palettes**. See [Report palettes](report-palettes.md).
- **Fonts:** in the builder, set **Heading Font** and **Body Font** under **⋯ → Styles**. See [Font pairs](font-pairs.md).
- **Brand colors** are one click away for any element: select it in the builder, open its color picker, and pick from the **Your brand colors** row.
