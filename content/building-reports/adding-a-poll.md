# Adding a poll

A poll asks your readers a question inside the report and shows the answers as a bar chart. It's the quickest way to turn a report from something people read into something they take part in.

Answers collect under **Poll Results** in that report's Analytics, where you can see them per person and export them.

## Adding a poll

1. In the builder, open the **Insert** bar and choose **Poll**.
2. Click the poll to open its editor.
3. Type your question and fill in the answers.
4. Click **Done**.

A new poll starts empty on purpose: no sample question, no sample answers. An unedited poll shows nothing at all to a reader, so a poll you forgot to finish can never reach a donor with placeholder wording in it.

You can also add a whole **Poll** section from **Add new section**, which gives the poll a page of its own rather than sitting it inside another block.

## Answers

A poll needs at least two answers and takes up to ten. Past ten the bars stop being readable stacked in a column, so the editor won't add more.

Each answer gets an accent color, seeded from a set that works together. You can change any of them.

> **Tip:** Keep answers short. Past about 32 characters an answer wraps to a second line and the layout moves the text above its bar instead of beside it. The editor warns you on the answer that's about to trigger it.

## Settings

Open the poll's settings panel to control how it behaves and how it looks.

**How it behaves**

- **Selection** — **Single choice** for one answer per reader, **Multiple choice** to let them pick several.
- **After Voting** — **Show everyone the results** reveals the full tally once someone answers. **Confirm the viewer's answer only** shows them what they chose and keeps everyone else's answers to you.
- **Question** — show or hide the question above the chart, for when the surrounding copy already asks it.

**How it reads**

- **Show Values As** — counts, percentages, or both.
- **Value Position** — after the bar, inside it, or hidden.
- **Answers/Options Text** — **Automatic** places each answer beside its bar and moves it above when it's too long. You can also force **Beside**, **Above**, or **Inside the bar**.

Font, text color, bar height, roundness, and spacing are all in the same panel, and brand kit colors appear in every color picker.

## What readers see

A reader picks an answer and the bars animate to the result straight away. Nothing waits on the network, so it feels instant even on a slow connection.

Votes are only recorded on the **published** report. Answering a poll while you're proofing in the builder, in preview, in print view, or in a PDF export does not count — a poll you clicked through while checking your work will never appear in your results.

## Seeing the results

Open the report's **Analytics** and choose the **Poll Results** tab. It appears on any report that contains a poll.

For each poll you get the question, the tally per answer, and a list of who answered. A reader who opened a [personalized link](../distribution-and-engagement/personalized-links.md) is named; anyone else shows as **Anonymous**. Use the export button on a poll to download its votes as a CSV.

> **Note:** Because a personalized link identifies the reader, poll answers on a personalized report are attributable to a named constituent. Say so in the question if the answer is something people would expect to give anonymously.
