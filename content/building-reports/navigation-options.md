# Navigation options

Published reports come with built-in navigation so readers can jump straight to what interests them — or simply scroll from top to bottom. This page covers what you can control.

## How report navigation works

Your report's **table of contents** — the ordered list of its sections — drives a navigation menu in the published report:

- On desktop, readers see a navigation bar of your sections.
- On mobile, the bar always collapses into a menu button at the top right of the screen.
- The current section is highlighted as readers move through the report.

You don't build this menu by hand; it follows your sections automatically. Renaming or [reordering sections](reordering-sections.md) updates it.

## Section titles are menu labels

Each section's title is its label in the navigation menu. Keep titles short and scannable — "Our Impact" navigates better than "A Look Back at Everything We Accomplished Together This Year." You can rename sections and change their thumbnails in **Report settings** (the gear in the top bar) on the **Sections** tab.

## Navigation settings

Open **Report settings** and go to the **Navigation** tab:

- **Navigation title** — the label on the navigation bar. Leave it blank for "Sections"; some reports prefer "Table of Contents."
- **Show section navigation bar** — turn the bar off entirely, on both desktop and mobile.
- **Navigation Bar Position** — place the bar at the **Top**, **Left**, **Right**, or **Bottom** of the page on desktop.
- **Section thumbnails on the right (right-to-left)** — for reports in right-to-left languages.
- **Section navigation footer** — the Previous / Next links at the bottom of every section. **Standard** shows two full-bleed image cards; **Slim bar** is a single short strip with a thumbnail beside each title. Republish to apply a change to your live report.

Every section in your report appears in the navigation; there's no setting to hide an individual section from it.

## Tips

- **Aim for 5–9 menu items.** If your report has fifteen sections, consider combining the supporting ones so the menu shows just the landmarks.
- **Check the mobile menu.** Long section titles truncate sooner on phones — preview in mobile view. See [Mobile optimization](mobile-optimization.md).
