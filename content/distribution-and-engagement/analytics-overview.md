# Analytics overview

Publishing your report isn't the end of the story. Storyraise Analytics helps you understand what happens next: who is reading, what holds their attention, what inspires action, and where a personal follow-up might make a difference.

> **Tip:** Prefer to see it? [Watch the two-minute tour and explore the interactive demo](/storyraise-analytics/) — the real Analytics page, with sample data you can click through.

## What's new

Storyraise now gives you a clearer picture of reader engagement. Instead of a running page-view total, Analytics distinguishes between individual readers and repeat visits, and shows how people move through your report.

- **Deeper engagement insights.** Constituents Opened, Constituent Returns, Views, and Avg. Time Spent; Where The Clicks Go; How They Read and Section Retention; Top Visit Times and Visits Over Time; and star-rated Engagement Scores with intent badges for personalized links.
- **More accurate measurement.** Repeat page loads are no longer counted as separate readers, and your team's own views are excluded when Storyraise recognizes a signed-in teammate.
- **Named reader signals.** With [personalized links](personalized-links.md), you can see which constituents are returning, reading deeply, and taking action.

## Why your numbers may look lower

> **Lower isn't worse.** Your new totals may look smaller than older page-view counts. That doesn't mean performance declined — it means the measurement got more honest.

Older reports counted repeat page loads, so one person opening a report four times added four to the total. Analytics de-duplicates that activity to give you a truer picture of reach and engagement. Compare newer reports with other newer reports, not with legacy totals — see [Analytics for older reports](analytics-for-older-reports.md).

## Start with your Home tab

Your **Home** tab gives you a quick look at reader activity across your whole organization:

- **Most read** — which reports are attracting the most attention.
- **Reader activity** — 30 days of reading across your organization.
- **Who's Been Reading** — named constituents who opened personalized reports.

Treat the Home tab as a *weekly* check-in. It's how you spot a report that's still gaining traction, a piece worth resharing, or a constituent who may be ready for a personal touch.

## Then open a report's Analytics tab

For the deeper story on a single report, select **Analytics** in the sidebar and choose the report. You'll find two views:

- **The Numbers** — where readers are, when they visit, what they click, how they move through your report, and where they stop. See [Understanding report metrics](understanding-report-metrics.md).
- **The Story** — everything brought together in a plain-English summary you can download as a PDF, including key takeaways, people worth contacting, and recommended next steps. It's ready to read aloud in a staff meeting or hand to your board.

If the report contains a poll or a reader feedback card, you'll also see **Poll Results** and **Reader Feedback** tabs, each with a **Download CSV** button. The **Poll Results** tab is called **Polls & Quizzes** once the report has at least one [quiz](../building-reports/adding-a-quiz.md).

## Poll and quiz results

Each poll or quiz in the report gets a card with its question, how many people responded, and a bar for each answer. When a report has both, each card is labeled **Poll** or **Quiz**.

A quiz card adds:

- **How many got it right** — for example *18 of 24 answered correctly*, with a ring showing the percentage.
- **The most common other answer** — the answer most readers reached for instead, and how many chose it. It's worth a sentence in your next update.
- **The correct answer, ticked** on the bars.

![A quiz card on the Polls & Quizzes tab, with the percentage ring and the correct answer ticked](/assets/img/howto/quiz-dashboard.png)

Grading happens when you open the tab, so if you change a quiz's correct answer, every response is graded again against the new one.

**Who responded**

Click **Show who responded** to see each respondent, their answer, and when they answered. On a quiz, a **Result** column shows **Correct** or **Not quite**. A reader who opened a [personalized link](personalized-links.md) is named; anyone else shows as **Anonymous**.

- **Search** by name or answer.
- **Filter** a quiz to **All**, **Correct**, or **Not quite**, or a poll to one answer.
- **Scroll** for more — the list loads 25 at a time, and the count below it shows how many match.

![Searching quiz respondents, with the Not quite filter selected](/assets/img/howto/quiz-dashboard-search.png)

Use a filter as a follow-up list: the readers who answered *Not quite* are the ones who'll most appreciate hearing the full story.

**Download CSV** exports one row per answer, with the question, the answer, and the respondent's name and constituent ID. A quiz export adds two columns: **Correct** (Yes or No) and **Correct Answer**.

## How fresh the data is

- **A report's Analytics tab is near-live.** Reader activity shows up within minutes.
- **Home tab cards refresh overnight**, covering activity through the previous night. If today's numbers look flat on the Home tab, check the report's own Analytics tab.

Keep in mind that a visit is recorded once the reader *leaves* your report — so someone still reading right now won't appear yet. See [Tracking engagement](tracking-engagement.md) for the full picture of what is and isn't captured.
