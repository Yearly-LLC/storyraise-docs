# What is Storyraise Collect?

Storyraise Collect gathers the raw material your stories are made of. Build a form, share the link, and collect stories, thank-yous, photos, and data from your community — then put what you collect to work in your reports and videos.

## Start a collection

In the dashboard, open **Collections** in the sidebar and click **Add new** (or click **New collection** on Home). Storyraise Collect opens in a new tab with a fresh collection. Inside Collect, **Start new collection** does the same. Either way, you then choose what you want to collect:

- **Thank-yous from beneficiaries**
- **Stories from program leaders**
- **Stories from the field**
- **Stories from our community**
- **General information**
- **I want to upload information** — start from a CSV instead of a form

Each starting point pre-builds a sensible form you can customize.

## Build your form

The form editor gives you a full toolkit of field types: **Short text**, **Long text**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Choose one**, **Choose many**, **Dropdown list**, **Number**, **Date**, and **File upload** (photos, video, audio, or documents such as PDF, Word, and Excel files) — plus **Heading**, **Paragraph**, and **New Section** elements for structure. Every field can have its own label, placeholder, description, and required setting.

Longer forms can be split into **steps**: respondents see a progress bar ("Step 1 of 3"), can move back and forth, and can jump between sections. Drafts save automatically as people type, so a closed tab doesn't mean a lost story.

## What respondents experience

A clean, branded form with your logo and introduction — no account needed. If you send a personalized link and the person's email matches a row already in the collection (for example, from a CSV you imported), Storyraise **pre-fills their information** from that row, so a donor sharing a story doesn't have to retype their own name. See [Sharing your form](sharing-your-form.md).

## Work with what you collect

Responses land in a spreadsheet-style table you can actually work in:

- **Search** responses by keyword.
- **Edit any response** — click a cell to fix a typo or fill a gap, right in the table.
- **Tag responses to constituents** — click a response's **Constituents** cell to connect a submitted story to the donor or volunteer it came from.
- **Import CSV** to bring existing data into a collection (append, merge with matching rows, or replace).
- **Export CSV** to take everything with you.

## Where it fits

Collections exist to feed the rest of Storyraise — as the dashboard puts it: *store collections of information here to use in your reports and videos.* Gather beneficiary thank-yous in spring, and they're ready as testimonials in your fall [report](../getting-started/what-is-storyraise.md); collect field stories year-round and your annual report writes itself from real voices.
