Report folders

Folders group the reports on your dashboard — by year, by program, by campaign, or by whatever your team actually looks for. You can also limit a folder to named people, so work in progress stays off everyone else's dashboard.

Creating a folder

Click New report on the dashboard and choose Folder, then give it a name. If you don't have any folders yet, an Add new folder button sits where the folder grid will go.

Folders show the number of reports filed in each, and clicking one opens it.

Filing reports

On any report's ⋯ menu, choose Add to folder and pick the folder. The same menu moves a report between folders, and No folder takes it back out.

A report lives in one folder at a time.

Renaming and deleting

Both are on the folder's ⋯ menu.

Delete removes the folder, not the reports. Anything filed in it moves to No folder and you're told how many reports that will be before it happens. Nothing is lost, and nothing is unpublished.

Who can see a folder

By default every folder is visible to everyone on your team. You can restrict one to named people instead.

On the folder's ⋯ menu, choose Manage visibility, then pick:

  • Everyone on your team — how folders work by default.
  • Only the people I choose — hidden from everyone else's dashboard.

Choose the second and a member list appears. Search for teammates and check the ones who should keep access, then Save.

This is not a security control. It hides the folder and the reports inside it from other people's dashboards. It does not lock anything: anyone holding a report's link can still open it, and published reports stay public to the web. Use it to keep a work-in-progress out of the way, not to protect something sensitive.

What actually changes

  • The folder stops appearing on an excluded teammate's dashboard, along with the reports inside it. It isn't shown locked — it simply isn't there.
  • Those reports also drop out of their search results and out of the Add to folder menu.
  • If someone follows a direct link to a folder they can't see, they land back on the reports list.
  • Reports already in the folder stay exactly where they are. Nobody's report is moved, unpublished, or deleted.

Who always keeps access

You can't lock yourself out, and neither can anyone else. Three people always keep access to a folder, and appear in the list already checked and marked Always:

  • You, when you're the one setting the list
  • Account Owners
  • Whoever created the folder

Who can change it

Owners, Admins, and Editors can change who sees a folder — the same people who can rename or delete one. Viewers can't.

Every change is recorded in your organization's activity log, showing who changed a folder's visibility and who was added or removed.
