Brand kit

The brand kit is where your organization's look lives: your brand colors, your report palettes and font pairs, your logos and photos, and your saved blocks. Set it up once and every new report starts on brand. Reports you've already made keep their current look.

Set up your brand in 10 minutes

Open Toolkit → Brand Kit in the dashboard sidebar, then:

  1. Add your brand colors. Brand colors are your quick-pick colors: whenever you change the color of text, a background, a button, or anything else in the builder, the color picker shows them in a Your brand colors row, one click away. Click Add color, then drag in the color area and along the hue bar or type the hex code (with or without the #), and click Select. The square swatch next to the hue bar opens your browser's own color picker, which in Chrome includes an eyedropper.
  2. Create a report palette. A palette sets the starting colors of your reports: which colors go on buttons, dark sections, and light sections. Click New palette, choose the colors, and tick Use for new reports to make it your default palette. The default is preselected whenever someone creates a report, though they can pick a different one. Your first palette becomes the default automatically. See Report palettes.
  3. Add a font pair. Click New pair and choose a heading font and a body font. Your default pair is preselected when someone creates a report, the same way as the default palette. See Font pairs.
  4. Upload your logos on the Logos and assets tab.
  5. Create a report. Your default palette and font pair are already selected. See Starting a report on brand.

Finding your way around

Open the brand kit from the dashboard sidebar: Toolkit → Brand Kit. The page has three tabs:

  • Brand — brand colors, report palettes, font pairs, and uploaded font files.
  • Logos and assets — logos and brand photos for the report builder.
  • Block library — blocks you've saved from your reports to reuse.

Owners, Admins, and Editors can change the brand kit. Viewers can see the Brand tab but can't change brand colors, palettes, or font pairs — the Change, Add color, New palette, and New pair controls don't appear for them. See User roles and permissions.

What new reports start with

The top of the Brand tab shows a small sample report in your default palette and default font pair. Next to it, New reports start with names both.

Click Change next to Palette or Fonts to pick a different default from the palettes or pairs you've saved, or to start a new one.

The default is what's preselected when someone creates a report. They can still pick a different palette or pair for that report — see Starting a report on brand.

First visit: a drafted starting look

If you've added brand colors but haven't saved a palette or font pair yet, the top card offers a shortcut: a palette drafted from your colors, plus a suggested font pair.

  • Save as my brand saves the drafted palette and font pair as your defaults in one click.
  • Customize first opens the drafted palette in the palette editor so you can change it before saving.

The color chips above the buttons let you pick your main brand color. The draft then uses that color exactly as entered and only adjusts the others to keep text readable.

This card only appears until you save your first palette or pair.

Change or remove a brand color

Brand colors are separate from report palettes. A palette keeps its own copy of its colors, so editing or removing a brand color doesn't change your palettes.

Under each swatch, In 1 palette (or In 2 palettes, and so on) tells you how many saved palettes use that color. Not in a palette yet means none do.

Click a swatch to edit it. Adjust it in the picker or type the new hex, then click Save.

To delete a color, click Remove and confirm. Removing a brand color doesn't change any palette that uses it, and it never changes existing reports.

Report palettes and font pairs

These have their own pages:

  • Report palettes — which of your colors go on buttons, dark sections, and light sections, with contrast checks built in.
  • Font pairs — a heading font and a body font that belong together, plus uploading your own font files.

Logos and assets

The Logos and assets tab holds images your team reuses across reports.

  • Logos — click Add Logos (or the upload area) and choose one or more image files. PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best — see Image sizes and dimensions.
  • Photos — click Add Photos for brand photography you use often.

In the report builder, open the Assets tab in the sidebar, expand Your Logos or Your Images, and click one to use it. You don't need to upload it again. To remove an item, click the ✕ on its thumbnail. It's removed straight away, without a confirmation.

Tip: Upload a reversed (white) version of your logo as well as the full-color one. It's the one you'll want on photos and dark sections.

Block library

The Block library tab lists blocks your team has saved from reports, so a section you've designed once — a donor thank-you, a stats row, a footer — can be reused anywhere.

  • To save a block, select it in the report builder and click + (Add to library) on the toolbar, then name it.
  • To reuse one, switch the builder's Blocks panel to My Library and add it from there. Manage library in the same panel lets you review or delete saved blocks.
  • To rename or delete one, open the ⋯ menu on its card here and choose edit label or delete. Delete happens straight away, without a confirmation.

Saved blocks usually keep the colors and fonts they were saved with, so check a reused block against the report's palette.

What changes when you edit the brand kit

You change… New reports Existing reports
Brand colors Show up in element color pickers in the builder straight away Also show up in their color pickers. Colors already on the page don't change
Default palette or font pair Start with the new default Keep their current look
A palette's colors Use the updated palette Keep their own copy of the old colors
Logos and photos Available in the builder (your logo isn't placed automatically) Available in the builder
Uploaded font files Removing one also removes any pair that uses its family Reports using a removed font may lose it — see Font pairs

To bring a new palette into a report you've already built, switch palettes in the builder — see Report palettes.

Tips

  • Start with your brand guide's hex codes. Paste them in, then let the palette ideas show you combinations that are readable.
  • Fewer colors, used consistently, look more professional. Three or four brand colors are usually plenty.
  • Set the defaults before the team starts building. Everyone who creates a report gets your palette and fonts preselected.
