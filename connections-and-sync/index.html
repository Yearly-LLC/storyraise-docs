Your CRM and your reports, in step.

Connect Raiser's Edge NXT or Salesforce in a few minutes. Storyraise keeps your donors and their giving up to date on a daily or weekly schedule, and sends what each donor read back to their record.

    Raiser's Edge NXT

    Lists in, actions back

    • Who syncs: one Raiser's Edge NXT list you choose, with each constituent's name and email.
    • More data: Add data brings gifts across with amount, date, type, fund, campaign, and appeal.

    List Views in, Tasks back

    • Opens, how many reports, minutes read, and link clicks, in one line on the record.
    • One action or Task per donor. When they read more, it is updated, not repeated.
    • Only donors who came from that CRM, and only above the level of engagement you choose.

    When it runs

    • Save & send now writes it straight away.
    • After that, it goes out with every scheduled sync while Auto-sync is on.
    • Matched by each donor's own CRM record, so it lands on the right person.

    Questions teams ask

    Is Auto-sync on as soon as I connect?

    No. Your first sync runs when you choose a list and select Sync. Auto-sync is a switch in each connection's More menu, so you decide when scheduled syncs start and whether they run daily or weekly.

    What happens when I delete or merge a record in my CRM?

    Each sync adds new records and updates existing ones. It does not delete anyone from Storyraise, so a record removed in your CRM stays in Storyraise until you remove it. Managing connections explains your options.

    What access does Storyraise ask for?

    You connect through Blackbaud's or Salesforce's own sign-in, so Storyraise never sees your CRM password. Access is what that connection needs to read your constituents and, if you turn on write-back, to log actions or Tasks. Disconnecting deletes the credentials Storyraise stored and asks your CRM to revoke access. Security and data has the details.

    Does every contact come across?

    Every contact in the list or List Views you choose that has an email address. Email is how each donor receives their report, so contacts without one are skipped.

    Will write-back fill our CRM with duplicate activities?

    No. Each donor gets one Storyraise action or Task, and later sends update it in place. If someone deletes it in the CRM, the next send creates it again.

    Which systems support write-back?

    Raiser's Edge NXT and Salesforce. Bloomerang, Virtuous, DonorPerfect, Neon CRM, and the others sync into Storyraise the same way, with Auto-sync and Sync now. See every supported system.
